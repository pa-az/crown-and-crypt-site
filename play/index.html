Stars and hints

Win the field
Every knight still standing
Win fast, or on a Hold field keep every town

Each star counts on its own. Fast means by the field's par turn: miss it and the win tells you the turn to beat.

Hints show you a winning turn, two for each march. Spend one on a field and its hints stay free, but a win with a hint earns only the first star. In the tutorial they are always free.
