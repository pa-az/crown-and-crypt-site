The game

Crown & Crypt is a turn-based tactics game played on small 7x7 fields. Each field is a puzzle of position: shove the dead into water, drag them into fire, bring walls down on their heads, and hold the villages they come to burn.

Before you commit a blow, the board shows exactly what it will do: who is hurt, who is knocked back, who drowns. The dead are cunning, but you always know the cost of your move.

The road

  • Ten short lesson fields teach every knight and every foe.
  • Three marches of hard fields, each harder than the last.
  • Three stars on every field, and hints when you are stuck.
  • Painted art and an original score.
  • No account, no ads, plays offline. On iPhone, Android and PC.
