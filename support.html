Support

Found a bug, stuck on a field, or have a thought about the game? Write to mycreatz@outlook.com. Please say which device you play on and, for a bug, what you did just before it happened.

Questions

Where do I start?

Play the Tutorial from the menu. Its ten short fields teach each knight and each of the dead, one at a time.

How do stars work?

Every field has three: one for winning, one for keeping every knight standing, and one for winning within the field's turn limit. On a field where you hold ground, the third is for keeping every village whole.

What does a blow do before I make it?

Choose a knight and a target and the board shows the result first: the damage, where anyone is knocked, and who drowns or burns. What it shows is exactly what happens.

I am stuck on a field.

Tap the hint button beside Restart. Each march has five hints, and some fields carry a few of their own. The tutorial's hints are free.

How do I start over?

In Settings, "Forget the tutorial" resets the lessons, and "Forget the march" locks the road again and clears its stars and hints. Tap twice to confirm. Deleting the app also removes all progress, since it is kept only on your device.

Does my progress move to a new phone?

Not yet. Progress is stored only on the device you play on.

Crown & Crypt needs no account and no internet connection.
